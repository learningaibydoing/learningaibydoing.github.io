Facilitator notes

What to say. Real picture makers learn what a clean picture looks like, then start from random static and remove the noise in many small steps, guided by the words you type. Here the "learning" is simple counting over the room's drawings, so it is a toy version of the same idea.

Careful, Balanced, Adventurous. Careful gives the room\u2019s typical picture: each square takes the color most drawings agree on. Balanced and Adventurous let less common choices through, so pictures vary more.

Try this. Make a Sun with "Very closely", then run it again from new static and compare. Switch to "Loosely" and see pictures mix. Remove every House drawing and ask for a House. Then have the room draw a House and ask again. The painter can only make what it has seen.

Honest limits. Real systems use large neural networks trained on millions of images. This toy has no understanding of suns or houses. It only reuses patterns from the drawings here.
